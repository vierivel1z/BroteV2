# Academic demo checklist

PLAY_REQUIRED: perform this in the actual player before presenting.

1. Open the game; show Suyay, the valley and the single Salud del Valle bar.
2. Explain WASD/arrows and E. Approach the front of the nursery; collect one seedling.
3. Confirm the seedling counter increases; collecting at capacity5 shows Inventario lleno.
4. Visit an empty planting spot; press E once. One seedling is consumed and one tree appears with a small dust/leaf pop.
5. Observe Seedling to Young after5s, then Developed after another5s. Show the stronger leaf/pulse feedback without camera shake.
6. Confirm exactly+10 in Salud del Valle, smooth bar movement and nearby Ground restoration.
7. Repeat at all ten spots, returning to the nursery as needed. Reach100 and show Sector restaurado; props activate at20/40/50.
8. Demonstrate the scenic landscape is decorative: walk against its boundary and slide sideways; remain on Ground.

Before the demo: validate scene/prefabs, test Spot_08 from below, check resolution, clear unexpected Console errors, prepare a fresh session.
